# Unaided capstone: Release, restrict, or hold?

The cases below are original and fictional. Work independently, preserve the evidence locators, and submit an evidence dossier rather than asking a model to give a verdict.

A library board is reviewing a report assistant that claims “three of four reviewed exports were extracted correctly, so automated publication is ready.” It can draft a proposed report for human review; no public communication is authorized. The board is willing to continue a bounded internal pilot with staff review if the evidence supports it.

| M4 | 44 | 44 | 44 |

Two maintenance teams report these reviewed audit counts for requests needing escalation: North printed packets have 6 misses out of 24 needs; South tablet forms have 9 misses out of 18 needs. You do not know how the reference labels were collected. Three regularly collected forecast cohorts contained two, four and six events each out of ten reviewed windows; all were issued a probability of 0.40. There is no evidence that these cohorts are independent or representative of the next season.

The trace record says `read R21; propose report R21`. The separate registry read shows R22 with no publication. The assistant reports “published.” Nobody has shown an authorized public operation; ownership of the R22 change is unresolved.

## Independent response

1. Split the initial statement into a numeric claim, a scope claim, and a decision proposition. Cite the cells and independent trace or state supporting each disposition.
2. Compare baseline and variant correctness, coverage and paired changes. Record a timeout rather than treating it as a correct number or a data deletion.
3. Calculate the two observed miss rates. Explain what label or case-mix evidence is needed before interpreting the difference as caused by the workflow.
4. Calculate the pooled observed forecast frequency, then explain why a pooled match would not show that probabilities are equally useful in all three cohorts.
5. Reconcile the decision authority, observed trace and final state. State what remains unresolved and what must not be retried automatically.
6. Write one recommendation: release, restrict, or hold, with evidence, owner, stop condition and a falsifiable reopening condition.

## How to assess it

Score each category 0 (missing), 1 (present, incomplete) or 2 (reproducible and qualified): claim and scope; quantitative checks; causal restraint; independent state/permission check; decision plus reopening rule. Grade the initial unaided work. Do not award credit for treating “sent” as proof, removing failures invisibly, guessing why the registry changed, or reporting model-generated approval. The intended assessment is of this written exercise, not certification.

After submitting the initial response, you may optionally give the fictional packet to an assistant and ask for one precise counterexample to a conclusion. Confirm it on your own; save the critique and revision separately. Do not use real operational data.
